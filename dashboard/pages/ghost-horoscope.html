Sun, Moon & Rising — what they actually mean

☉ Sun sign
The "you" most people mean by your star sign — core identity and ego. Needs only your birth date; it doesn't move fast enough for time or place to matter.
☾ Moon sign
Your emotional inner world — how you actually feel and self-soothe, versus how you present. The Moon changes sign roughly every 2.5 days, so an accurate birth date and time genuinely matter here.
↑ Rising sign (Ascendant)
The mask you present, first impressions, how others read you on meeting. Changes roughly every 2 hours — the most sensitive of the three to a precise birth time and location.
