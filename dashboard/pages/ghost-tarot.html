How this works, if you're new to it

The deck. 78 cards split into the Major Arcana (22 cards, 0–21, the big archetypal life themes — The Fool through The World) and the Minor Arcana (56 cards across four suits — Wands, Cups, Swords, Pentacles — covering the day-to-day texture of a situation, similar in spirit to a playing-card deck's four suits).

Upright vs. reversed. Every card drawn also gets an independent coin-flip for orientation. Reversed doesn't strictly mean "opposite" — it's usually read as the card's energy being blocked, internalised, or still developing, rather than fully expressed.

The suits, roughly. Wands → action, ambition, creative drive. Cups → emotion, relationships, intuition. Swords → thought, conflict, truth-telling. Pentacles → the material world — money, work, the body.

Reading a spread. Each position in a spread carries its own question (see the note above the cards once you draw) — the same card means something different in a "past" slot than in an "outcome" slot.
